Answer: The team match algorithm scores a candidate's gap coverage by:
1. Identifying skills the candidate has that the team does not already possess.
2. Following directed `COMPLEMENTS` relationships from those candidate skills to the team's skills.
3. Summing the `strength` attribute values of those `COMPLEMENTS` edges (`sum(coalesce(r.strength, 0))`) for the candidate.

- [ ] candidates with a score of zero are dropped and the top N (20 by default) are returned

### fs-02 run 3: How does the Twitch client handle rate limits and failed requests?

Answer: In `Twitch.get`, requests are attempted up to 8 times:

- **Network exceptions (`RequestException`)**: Retries after sleeping for `min(60, 5 * (attempt + 1))` seconds.
- **401 Unauthorized**: Re-authenticates using `self._set_auth(force=True)` and retries.
- **429 (Rate Limit) & Status >= 500**: Sleeps until the time specified in the `Ratelimit-Reset` header (plus 1 second buffer) and retries.
- **Low Rate Limit Remaining (< 10)**: Sleeps until the `Ratelimit-Reset` time even after a successful response.
- If all 8 attempts fail, it raises a `RuntimeError`.

- [x] it retries up to 8 times
- [x] on 429 or a 5xx error it waits until the rate limit reset time
- [x] on 401 it refreshes the token

### sh-06 run 3: How does synergyhack stop two users from taking the last slot in a team?

- [x] Redis HINCRBY on the filled counter, which is atomic
- [x] if filled goes above capacity it is rolled back
- [x] claimSlot returns false

### fs-01 run 2: How is the fit score of a streamer computed and what are its parts?

Answer: The fit score is calculated as the rounded sum of five weighted components (totaling 0–100):
1. Engagement (`eng`): Based on `engagement_pct`, worth up to 30 points.
2. Consistency (`consistency`): Ratio of `days_seen_live` to `sweep_days`, worth up to 20 points.
3. Giveaway and sponsor signals (`signal`): 0, 10, 15, or 20 points based on `does_giveaways`, `has_sponsor_signal`, and `brand_count`.
4. Location confidence (`loc`): 15 points for high, 11 for medium, 6 for low, or 0 for none.
5. Audience size (`size`): 15 points if `avg_viewers` is between 20 and 80, 10 points if within minimum/maximum thresholds, or 3 points otherwise.

- [ ] a score from 0 to 100 with the breakdown kept visible
- [x] engagement up to 30, consistency 20, giveaway and brand signal 20, location 15, audience size 15
